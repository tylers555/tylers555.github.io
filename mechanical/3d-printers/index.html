Dawg Anet A8

Dawg

I am in the process of designing my own 3D printer from scratch. I am currently working on finishing it's CAD design, and will then move on to assembly and testing.

The initial design of the machine will use regular off the shelf electronics and parts that I have available from other printers I have. However, I plan to use this machine as a test platform for numerous modifications, such as a pick-and-place attachment.

Furthermore, I hope to eventually designed custom servo motors from BLDC motors that I can then use to control the printer alongside custom electronics. However, this is a project for the future.

Anet A8

My first 3D printer was the basic Anet A8, which came in a kit you had to assemble. It is a bed slinger style printer that worked alright out of the box. However, I sought to maximize the performance of this printer by installing a number of modifications on it and by running Klipper to maximize the performance of this machine.

This printer has outgrown it's use and it's parts will be salvaged for Dawg.
