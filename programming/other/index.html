Honeywell Devil's Invent

The project my team and I came up with was called APIARI and would utilize AI to detect skill deficiencies at Honeywell or other companies and then determine effective paths to filling in those deficiencies. It would also allow managers to choose the best people for projects, be it either to further develop skills or to meet tight deadlines.

I programmed the demo app for this project, which is capable of sorting and tracking and performing basic analysis of employee skills.
